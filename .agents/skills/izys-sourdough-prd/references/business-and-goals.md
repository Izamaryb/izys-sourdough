# Business & Goals

## Product Overview

### Purpose

A **mobile-first preorder website** for a small in-home sourdough bakery that
allows local customers to:

* Browse available breads
* View ingredients and allergen info
* Preorder limited inventory
* Select a pickup time slot
* Receive pickup instructions and reminders

The system supports **small-batch artisan baking**, prevents overproduction,
and ensures a smooth pickup experience.

### Business Model

* Bake Day: Tuesday
* Pickup Day: Wednesday
* Order Cutoff: 48 hours before pickup
* Edit/Cancel Window: up to 36 hours before pickup
* Inventory: fixed per bake (e.g., 10 loaves total)
* Customers can order multiple loaves per order
* Pickup slots limit **people**, not loaves
* When inventory = 0 → ordering closes
* Optional extras available for late/day-of orders

## Goals

### Business Goals

* Sell out weekly inventory
* Maintain manageable production
* Build repeat local customers
* Minimize waste

### User Goals

* Order bread in under 2–3 minutes
* Clearly understand pickup process
* Have a simple, frictionless experience

## Target Users

* Local customers in Zephyrhills, Dade City, Wesley Chapel, New Tampa
* Ages 30–60+
* Mobile-first users
* Value clarity, simplicity, and quality
